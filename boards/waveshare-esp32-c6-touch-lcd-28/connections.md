# Waveshare ESP32-C6-Touch-LCD-2.8

ESP32-C6, 16 MB flash, no PSRAM. The ST7789 240 × 320 display and microSD card share SPI: SCK GPIO0, MOSI GPIO1, MISO GPIO8, display CS GPIO11, display DC GPIO10, and SD CS GPIO23. I²C uses SDA GPIO6 and SCL GPIO7. CST3530 touch is at 0x58 with interrupt GPIO18. The CH32V003 expander at 0x24 controls touch reset (EXIO0), display reset (EXIO1), backlight PWM and battery ADC. The expander must release and pulse the display reset before display initialization. The display and backlight have been observed working, and the SD card mounted during bring-up. Touch navigation and alignment have been confirmed in all orientations; held touches trigger once and separate taps register. Display rotation changes and SD directory browsing work. Power-off turns off the backlight and enters deep sleep. A connected battery reported a plausible 87%; percentage is estimated from the expander ADC using the vendor’s nominal voltage conversion and has not been calibrated against a meter. The board also runs on battery power. Installing an app from the SD card has been tested. Reset is required to restart after power-off. The proposed LauncherHub category is `waveshare-esp32-c6-touch-lcd-280`; the online installer remains disabled until that category and compatible firmware are available.
